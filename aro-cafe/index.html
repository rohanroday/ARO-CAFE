The name

Aro is what you shout across the street when you spot a friend.

It's a Tibetan hello, warm and a little loud. We opened on MG Marg on June 10, 2025 to give that feeling a home: good coffee in the morning, good food all day, and a good crowd at night.

Good to know

Before you come over.

Anything else? Send us a message on Instagram. Someone from the A-team will reply.

Is Aro a cafe or a bar?

Both. Coffee and brunch by day, cocktails and music by night. The sign says Bar and Vibes, and we mean it.

What time are you open?

9am to 9pm. Check our Instagram for holiday hours and special nights.

Can I get Aro delivered?

Yes. We deliver across Gangtok on Dash: sushi, ramen, matcha treats and more.

Do I need to book a table?

No, just walk in. Coming with a big group or planning a party? Message us on Instagram first and we'll get a space ready.

Do you host events?

We do. We threw Gangtok's first Run Rave, a 5K run with DJ sets after. Follow our Instagram to catch the next one.

Where exactly are you?

On MG Marg, Gangtok's main walking street. Look for the white and blue Aro cafe sign with flowers along the front.
